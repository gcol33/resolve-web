RESOLVE plot predictor

Upload the species lists of vegetation plots and get back what the plots are missing: EUNIS habitat, altitude, slope, plot area, survey year and location. The models were trained on 1.9 million European plots from the European Vegetation Archive and run here, in your browser: your files never leave your computer.

1. Your plots

One row per species record, with a plot identifier and a species name (World Flora Online spelling). A cover value (percent) is used when present. Coordinates, year and month can sit in the same table, repeated on every record of a plot, or in a separate table with one row per plot.

Columns

2. What to predict

Plots with coordinates use the models that also read location, year and month; plots without use the species-only models. Every prediction is the combined result of five independently trained models, with their spread.
