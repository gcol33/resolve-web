RESOLVE plot predictor

Predicts the EUNIS habitat, altitude, slope, plot area, survey year and location of vegetation plots from their species lists. The models were trained on 1.9 million European plots from the European Vegetation Archive. They run in your browser; your data are not uploaded anywhere.

What to predict

Plots with coordinates use the models that also read location, year and month; plots without use the species-only models. Every prediction combines five independently trained models, and gives their spread.

One plot

Type or paste the species of a plot, one per line. A cover value in percent may follow the name. The list below is a beech forest in the Vienna Woods; press Predict to try it.

Coordinates, year and month are optional; leave them empty if unknown.

Many plots

Upload a table with one row per species record (CSV, semicolon- or tab-separated). Coordinates, year and month can be columns of the same table, repeated on every record of a plot, or of a second table with one row per plot. The columns are found by name:
